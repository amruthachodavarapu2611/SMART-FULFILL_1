About Batch Allocation

🎯 How it works:

  • Analyzes all pending orders for the selected product
  • Prioritizes based on order priority levels and deadlines
  • Intelligently resolves inventory conflicts
  • Allocates stock to maximize fulfillment
  • Logs all allocation decisions with reasons
  • Automatically reserves inventory for allocated orders

📌 Example Scenario:

Available Stock: 10 units

Order 1 (CRITICAL): Needs 10 units → Allocated: 10

Order 2 (LOW): Needs 5 units → Backordered: 5

The system prioritizes critical orders first, ensuring urgent shipments are fulfilled before lower priority ones.
